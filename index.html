Plop

How to play

Turn the starting word into POOP, one letter at a time.

  • Each guess must change exactly one letter and be a real word.
  • Letters stay in their positions. No rearranging.
  • A tile turns brown when that letter is right for POOP.
  • There is no limit on guesses. Fewer is better.

Example

A new Plop arrives every day at midnight UTC. Share your result and see who found the shortest ladder.
